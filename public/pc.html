GAME COMPATIBILITY & QUICK START GUIDES

1. STEAM & RACING SIMS

Works natively with Assetto Corsa, BeamNG.drive, Euro Truck Simulator 2, Forza Horizon. Steam detects "Xbox 360 Controller" out of the box. Simply bind steering to Left Stick X and pedals to RT/LT.

2. EMULATORS (PS2 / PS3 / RETRO)

In RPCS3, PCSX2, Dolphin, DuckStation, or RetroArch: go to Controllers → Input Device → Select "Microsoft X-Box 360 pad". Full dual-stick and trigger analog support.

3. KEYBOARD-ONLY GAMES

For TrackMania, GTA V, Flash racers: click "Keyboard WASD Mode" above. Your phone tilt and pedal touches automatically press W, A, S, D, and Space at the OS kernel level!

NATIVE PC GAMES INTEGRATION

Gyropad includes a kernel-level Linux virtual gamepad driver via /dev/uinput.

To pipe your phone gyroscope directly into Steam games (Assetto Corsa, Euro Truck Simulator 2, BeamNG.drive, Dirt Rally): 

sudo python3 virtual_gamepad.py

Your system will immediately register a native Xbox 360 controller device with zero configuration required.
